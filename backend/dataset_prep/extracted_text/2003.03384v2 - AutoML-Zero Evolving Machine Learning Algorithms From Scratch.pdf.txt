Theobjective wastoreducehumanbiasinthesearchspace,inthehope thatthiswilleventuallyleadtonewMLconcepts. Asastart, wedemonstratedthepotentialofthisresearchdirectionby constructinganovelframeworkthatrepresentsanMLalgorithmasacomputerprogramcomprisedofthreecomponent functions(Setup,Predict,Learn).
