INNA can avoid parasitic oscillations and produce acceleration; a first illustration of the behavior of the induced dynamics is given in Figure 1 for a simple non-smooth and non-convex function in R2. The rest of the paper is organized as follows. INNA is introduced in details in Section 2 and its convergence is established in Section 3.
