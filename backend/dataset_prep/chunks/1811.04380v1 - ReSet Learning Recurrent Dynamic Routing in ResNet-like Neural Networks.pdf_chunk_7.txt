Conclusion In this work, we introduced a ReSet layer that performs dynamic routing through the set of independent Computational Units (transformations). The proposed model achieved better classification results compared with the ResNet-38 model, having a comparable number of parameters. The model learned to separate paths of images from different classes and produced separate Computational Units for the final stage of the network to refine its predictions. The obtained results open a wide range of possible applications of the proposed mechanismofdynamicrecurrentroutingwithReSet. Forexample,ReSetcouldbeusedinNatural Language Processing, where one would expect the ReSet to process different parts of the sentence with different Computational Units.
