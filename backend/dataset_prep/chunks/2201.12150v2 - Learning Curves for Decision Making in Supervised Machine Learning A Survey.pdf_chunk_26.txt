In this section, we describe the learning curve model according to the notion of budget, using the variable b to avoid having to distinguish between sample sizes n or iterations t. In the spirit of Sec. 3, we use the notation f(a,b) to refer to the original random variable that generates observations of the performance of learner a at budget b (independently of whether f here is a sample-wise curve with b being the sample size or whether f is an iteration-wise curve with some implicit sample size n and b being the iteration).
