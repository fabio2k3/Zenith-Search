Accordingly, in this EML process, we identify the best k for clustering using silhouette coefficient and elbow diagram methods. Silhouette coefficient = max s(k). Where s(k) represents the mean s(i) over all data points in the trade dataset for a specific number of clusters k. The k-means model is developed for k=2 to k=20, “powerful” country clusters are then used to create better trade predictions/boosting. We aim to find the most appropriate Sum of Square Errors (SSE).
