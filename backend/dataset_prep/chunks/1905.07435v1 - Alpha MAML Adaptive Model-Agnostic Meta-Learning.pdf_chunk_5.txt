Implementation details The network has four modules with 3×3 convolutions and 64 filters. This is followed by batch normalization, a ReLU activation, and strided convolutions. The final output is fed into a softmax layer. The images are downsampled to 28×28, and the dimensionality of the last hidden layer is 64.
