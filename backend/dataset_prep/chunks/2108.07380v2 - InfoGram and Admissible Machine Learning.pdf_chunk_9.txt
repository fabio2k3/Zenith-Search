We end this section with a general remark: when applying machine learning algorithms in scientific applications, it is of the utmost importance to design models that can clearly explain the ‘why and how’ behind their decision-making process. We should not forget that scientists mainly use machine learning as a tool to gain a mechanistic understanding, so that they can judiciously intervene and control the system.
